From initial hobby training to professional degree certifications, our structured curriculum guides every milestone.

Indian Classical Dance

Kathak Classical Dance

Explore the lyrical beauty of Jaipur and Lucknow Gharana traditions. Students master rhythmic footwork (Tatkar), pirouettes (Chakkars), expressive gestures (Mudras), and dramatic storytelling (Abhinaya).

  • Age: Kids (5+ yrs), Teens & Adults
  • Levels: Beginner to Praveen (M.Mus Equivalent)
  • Annual Ghungroo Poojan & Stage Recitals
  • Prayag Sangeet Samiti Examination Prep
Weekend & Weekday Batches
Indian Percussion

Tabla Percussion Training

Learn the mathematical elegance and profound sound of the classical Indian drum pair. Focuses on hand positioning, clarity of bols, improvisation in Teental, Jhaptal, and stage accompaniment.

  • Age: 6+ yrs to Seniors
  • Solo Playing (Kayda, Rela, Tukda, Chakkardar)
  • Accompaniment for Vocal, Dance, & Harmonium
  • Personalized 1-on-1 Riyaz Guidance
Prathama to Visharad
Instrumental & Melody

Harmonium & Keyboard

Gain effortless agility across the keyboard and bellows. Master traditional raag-based lehras, devotional bhajans, ghazals, patriotic tunes, and semi-classical melodies with correct fingering techniques.

  • Age: Kids & Adults (Beginners Welcome)
  • Classical Swara, Thaat, & Alankar Drills
  • Bhajan, Kirtan & Light Music Accompaniment
  • Notation reading & Ear Training
Beginner to Advanced
Classical Vocal

Hindustani Classical Vocal

Cultivate your natural voice through the sacred science of Indian vocal music. Under the guidance of Mr. Lal Babu Rai, learn voice culture, breath control, swara sthan, raga bandish, and emotive expression.

  • Age: All Ages (Child to Senior Enthusiast)
  • Morning Riyaz & Breath Modulation
  • Khayal, Tarana, Dhrupad, & Semi-Classical
  • Stage Confidence & Microphone Technique
Prathama to Praveen
Fine Arts

Fine Arts & Drawing

Unleash the visual artist within. Comprehensive studio training spanning pencil sketching, freehand drawing, shading, perspective, watercolor landscapes, and colorful acrylic canvas masterpieces.

  • Age: 4+ yrs (Children, Teens & Hobbyists)
  • Pencil, Charcoal, Watercolor, & Acrylics
  • Annual Art Exhibition & Competitions
  • Prayag Sangeet Samiti Fine Arts Certification
All Skill Levels
Government Recognized

Prayag Sangeet Samiti Examination Tiers

Progress systematically through recognized grades that count towards school extra-curricular points and professional diplomas.

1

Prathama (Years 1 - 2)

Fundamental swara, laya, initial ragas, talas, and basic mudras. Solid foundation for beginners.

2

Madhyama (Years 3 - 4)

Intermediate level focusing on intricate compositions, improvisation, and stage presentation.

3

Visharad (Year 5 - 6)

Equivalent to a Bachelor’s Degree (B.Mus) in Music or Dance. Advanced mastery and solo performance.

4

Praveen (Post-Graduate)

Master's level degree (M.Mus Equivalent) for aspiring Gurus, researchers, and concert artists.
