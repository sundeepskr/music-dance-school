Our Heritage

A Dedicated Sanctuary of Indian Arts & Culture

Aarohi Sangeetalaya – Music, Dance & Fine Arts Institute is a premier centre of artistic learning committed to nurturing young talent and preserving the sacred richness of Indian cultural traditions.

Established in 2012 by Mr. Lal Babu Rai, an accomplished composer, musician, and classical vocalist, the institute has steadily flourished into a trusted institution for hundreds of students seeking authentic arts education.

We believe that art is a transformative medium of self-expression that shapes character, focus, discipline, and lifelong confidence. Guided by experienced mentors in an inspiring and positive atmosphere, every student is encouraged to discover their unique creative voice.

Affiliation with Prayag Sangeet Samiti, Allahabad

Preparing students for recognized Prathama, Madhyama, Visharad (Degree), and Praveen (Master's) examinations.

Our Purpose

Our Mission & Core Pillars

Every class at Aarohi Sangeetalaya is rooted in four steadfast principles of excellence.

🎯 Solid Fundamentals

We emphasize strong foundation in swaras, talas, tatkar, and brushwork so students can conquer complex compositions with ease.

🌟 Creativity with Discipline

Structured riyaz routines that cultivate patience, mindfulness, and creative thinking across every age group.

📜 Certified Credentials

Comprehensive exam preparation for Prayag Sangeet Samiti, empowering students with government-recognized qualifications.

🎤 Stage Confidence

Frequent internal baithaks, workshops, and grand annual auditorium concerts to help students overcome stage fright.
